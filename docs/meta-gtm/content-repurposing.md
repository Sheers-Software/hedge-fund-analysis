# Long-form → organic + paid content engine

> **The asset.** One ~22-min walkthrough of ApexAlpha — you on camera + real screens (type ticker →
> verdict → memo → valuation → charts → Intelligence). Real face + real UI = the highest-trust, most
> compliant creative we have (resolves the synthetic-presenter problem entirely).
>
> **The principle.** Don't post the 22-min and move on. Atomize it: one **pillar** → many **clips** →
> many **micro-assets**. Every clip serves twice — **organic reach** *and* the raw material for **Meta
> paid creative velocity** (the #1 CAC lever) plus **video-view retargeting audiences**. Paid stays
> Meta (per the playbook); organic is the new owned layer that lowers blended CAC and feeds the funnel.

Binds everything: Special Ad Category — no returns / "get rich" / performance promises; "Research &
education — not investment advice." on every asset; real screens only.

## 3. Clipping strategy (how to cut)

- **Auto-then-curate:** run the 22-min through an AI clipper — **Opus Clip** or **Klap** (auto-finds
  hooks, reframes to 9:16, adds captions, scores virality) — to get 20–30 candidates fast. Then
  human-pick the 8–15 that match the hook map and re-polish in **CapCut** (brand captions, end-card, VO
  if re-voicing). Descript (text-based editing) is great for precise trims off the transcript.
- **Hook-first, always:** lead with the payoff/result, not the setup. First frame = motion or a bold
  claim ("This stock is 40% below fair value — here's how I know"). Cut the first 3s ruthlessly.
- **One idea per clip.** If it needs two sentences to set up, it's a mid-form, not a short.
- **Captions mandatory** (sound-off), bold, bottom-third, Reels safe area. Accent-highlight the key word.
- **Length:** 15–30s converts best for cold; 30–45s for the demo/tutorial clips.
- **Reframe to 9:16**: your face in a corner bubble + the screen as the main panel (Opus/CapCut auto
  speaker-track), OR Ken-Burns the screen with your face lower-third.
- **Spec:** 1080×1920, H.264, mp4, 30fps, captions burned in.

## 4. Turning the video into other formats

- **Mid-form tutorials (3–6 min):** split the walkthrough by feature → "How to value any stock in 5
  min", "Reading an AI research memo", "Bull/Base/Bear in practice". YouTube + LinkedIn native. Evergreen,
  searchable, builds authority.
- **Carousels (IG / LinkedIn):** screenshot the key frames → 6–8 slide swipe ("5 numbers that tell you
  if a stock's overvalued"). Static, high-save, algorithm-friendly.
- **Blog / SEO article:** clean the transcript into "How to tell if a stock is overvalued (with AI)" —
  ranks for buyer search, links to `/app?check=`. Repurpose into the funnel's top.
- **Email nurture:** chop into a 3–5 email mini-course for the list (signup→tripwire→annual nudge).
- **X / LinkedIn text + clip:** thread the thesis ("I valued NVDA in 60 seconds — here's the process")
  with C1/C4 attached. The 35–60 / $75k buyer lives on X-finance and LinkedIn.
- **Audiogram / podcast:** strip the audio (your talk track) → a waveform-captioned audio clip; or a
  full podcast episode. Low effort, new surface.
- **GIFs / quote cards:** the verdict moment as a looping GIF for embeds; pull one-liners onto branded
  cards (use the `static-ads.html` style).

## 5. Organic distribution

| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | long-form hub + SEO + Shorts | 1 pillar + 2–3 mid-form / wk; Shorts daily-ish |
| **Instagram / Facebook (Reels)** | in-ecosystem organic; warms Meta pixel | 4–7 Reels / wk |
| **TikTok** | reach test (buyer skews older — secondary) | repost the Reels |
| **LinkedIn** | authority for the 35–60 / $75k buyer | 3–5 / wk (clips + carousels + text) |
| **X (FinTwit)** | high-intent finance community | daily (threads + clips) |
| **Blog/email** | SEO + owned nurture | 1 article / wk, email on cadence |

Organic ≠ free leads day one — it compounds. Its near-term payoff is **data** (which hooks win) and
**audiences** (viewers to retarget).

## 6. Paid strategy (Meta) — clips feed the machine

1. **Winning clips become ad creative.** Whatever earns the best organic retention/saves → run as Meta
   ads into the existing funnel (free verdict → account → $7 → annual). High creative velocity from one
   shoot = the cheapest CAC lever.
2. **Boost organic winners** as the cheapest test: a Reel with strong organic hold, put $20–50 behind it
   before producing net-new.
3. **Retargeting audiences the content builds:**
   - **Video-viewers** (25%/50%/75% of any clip) → warm Custom Audiences → retarget with the tripwire.
   - **Page/IG engagers** → retargeting ladder.
   - **Purchasers** → Special Ad Audience seed (no classic Lookalikes — finance category).
4. **Compliance binds the paid cuts harder than organic:** no performance claims, not-advice line,
   real screens, money-**back** guarantee OK. (Organic has slightly more latitude but keep the same
   spirit — don't promise returns.)

## 7. The flywheel

```
 22-min pillar ──► 8–15 clips ──► organic post (free reach + which-hook-wins data)
        │                              │
        │                              ├─► winners ──► Meta paid creative (cheap CAC, high velocity)
        │                              └─► viewers ──► video-view Custom Audiences ──► retargeting
        └─► mid-form + blog + carousels + email ──► SEO + owned nurture ──► funnel top
```

Organic finds the cheapest hook for free; paid scales it; viewers become a retargeting pool that drops
CAC — all governed by the same **LTV : CAC ≥ 3:1** gate and kill-switch (see conversion-tracking.md).

## 8. Measurement

| Layer | Watch | Good signal |
|---|---|---|
| Shorts (organic) | 3-sec & avg view %, saves, shares | high retention = a paid-ad candidate |
| Mid-form/YouTube | watch time, subs, search impressions | evergreen discovery |
| Meta paid | cost per verdict / signup / **annual purchase** | CAC ÷ $560 LTV ≥ 3:1 |
| Retargeting | cost per tripwire from video-viewers | cheaper than cold |
| Blog/email | organic traffic → `/app?check=`, email→tripwire | owned funnel lift |

Promote a clip from organic → paid only once it shows retention; cut paid the moment CAC breaks 3:1.

## 9. First-pass action plan
1. Upload the trimmed 22-min to **YouTube** (SEO title/description, link to `apexalpha.app`).
2. Auto-clip it (Opus/Klap) → curate **8–15** shorts to the hook map (§2) → polish in CapCut.
3. Post the hero (C1/H3) + C2/C4 organically across Reels/Shorts/TikTok/LinkedIn/X; watch retention.
4. Cut 2–3 mid-form tutorials + 1 blog from the transcript.
5. Feed the 2–3 best-retaining clips into Meta as ad variants; stand up video-view retargeting audiences.
6. Log winners back here; the product/creative bends toward what converts cheapest.
